# Friendsmith – Craft upgrades for your guild and your alts

*Text für die CurseForge-Projektseite (Beschreibung). Englisch zuerst, weil die meisten Besucher Englisch lesen, darunter Deutsch.*

---

**Who in my guild can craft me something better – and who can I help?**

Friendsmith looks at the gear everyone is wearing and the recipes everyone knows, and tells you which crafted items would be an upgrade for whom. Made for craft-locked guilds on WoW Forever, but works for any guild.

### Features
- **For me** – see who in your guild (or which of your alts) can craft you or your alts an upgrade.
- **I can craft** – see who you can help with your professions: your alts and your guildmates.
- **Upgrades next to your profession window** – "Upgrades for you", "Upgrades for <alt>", "Upgrades for <guildmate>" from the profession you have open. Click to open the recipe.
- **Spec-aware** – once you've spent talent points, items are rated by your spec's stats (random suffixes like "of the Monkey" included). Before that, item level is used. You can override it per character.
- Only gear the character can actually use: armor class, weapon skills, level, no caster gear for warriors and rogues. Bind-on-pickup items are only suggested to the crafter themselves.
- **One click to ask** – whisper a crafter with the item link already filled in, or offer your craft to a guildmate.
- Item tooltips show "craftable by …" and "upgrade for …".
- Data is shared invisibly through the guild addon channel with everyone running Friendsmith.
- Broker launcher (Titan Panel, ChocolateBar, …), minimap button and addon compartment entry.

### Getting started
1. Log in once on every character so Friendsmith knows their gear.
2. Open each profession window once (all categories expanded) so your recipes are recorded.
3. Ask your guild to install Friendsmith – syncing happens automatically on login.
4. Type `/fsmith` or click the minimap button.

### Commands
`/fsmith` window · `/fsmith sync` sync with guild · `/fsmith scan` re-read open profession · `/fsmith diff 3` minimum improvement · `/fsmith minimap` toggle minimap button · `/fsmith help`

*Languages: English and German (follows your game client).*

## Deutsch

**Wer in meiner Gilde kann mir etwas Besseres craften – und wem kann ich helfen?**

Friendsmith schaut sich an, was alle tragen und welche Rezepte alle kennen, und zeigt, welche herstellbaren Teile für wen ein Upgrade wären. Gemacht für Craftlocked-Gilden auf WoW Forever, funktioniert aber in jeder Gilde.

- **Für mich:** wer dir oder deinen Twinks ein Upgrade craften kann.
- **Ich kann craften:** wem du mit deinen Berufen helfen kannst – Twinks und Gilde.
- **Leiste am Berufsfenster** mit „Upgrades für dich / <Twink> / <Gildenmitglied>“.
- **Bewertung nach Spec** (inkl. Zufallswerten), ohne Talente nach Itemlevel.
- Anfrage oder Angebot per Flüstern mit einem Klick, Hinweise im Tooltip, Broker- und Minimap-Button.

Loslegen: jeden Charakter einmal einloggen, jedes Berufsfenster einmal öffnen, Gilde installieren lassen, `/fsmith`.
