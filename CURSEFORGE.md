# Friendsmith – Your guild's crafting & helping hub

*Text für die CurseForge-Projektseite (Beschreibung, Editor auf Markdown stellen). Englisch zuerst, darunter Deutsch. Stand: 0.13.0.*

---

**Who can craft me something better – and who can I help?**

Friendsmith brings your guild together around crafting. It knows what everyone wears, which recipes everyone has and what's in the guild bank – and shows you where you can help each other. It's made for players who'd rather not ask: Friendsmith asks for them.

Made for WoW Forever and craft-locked guilds, but it works in any guild. Friends outside your guild can join in too.

### Gear upgrades
- **For me** – who in your guild, among your friends or alts can craft you an upgrade.
- **I can craft** – who you can help with your professions.
- **Rated by spec** – once talents are spent, items are compared by your spec's stats (random suffixes like "of the Monkey" included); before that, by item level. Only gear the character can actually use. Bind-on-pickup items are only suggested to the crafter.
- **Next to your profession window** – "Upgrades for you / your alt / your guildmate" for the profession you have open. Click to open the recipe.

### Mats, guild bank & guild stock
- **Mats at a glance** – every upgrade shows the mats it needs: how many you have (bags and bank of all your characters), how many are in the guild bank, and what's missing.
- **Guild bank** – whenever someone with Friendsmith opens the guild bank, it's shared with the guild.
- **Mat requests** – *Ask for mats* creates a request your whole guild sees, and prepares a guild chat message (nothing is sent until you press Enter).
- **Guild stock** – officers set minimum amounts for potions, food, bandages and more. Everyone sees what's running low, who can craft it and which mats are needed.

### Professions & buff food
- **Profession help** – who is leveling a profession, how many points to the next big step and which mats they need. With **OneForAll** installed, its Skill-Up Helper route is used and shared with your guild.
- **Buff food** – who can cook the best "Well Fed" food in each category.

### Community
- **Silent wishes** – tick *Happy about help*, and your guild sees by itself what it could craft for you or which mats you need. You never have to ask.
- **Gentle hints** – after login, helpers see a few lines (only in their own chat) about who they could help.
- **Helper profile** – show what you're happy to help with: crafting, mats, dungeons, quests, gathering, questions or leveling together. The *Helpers* view tells shy players whom to ask, with a friendly whisper ready to go.
- **Welcome for newcomers** – new guild members are noticed, shared in the feed and listed with a *Whisper welcome* button.
- **Guild feed & thanks** – profession steps, level milestones, finished requests and thank-yous. Say thanks with one click – also right after a trade.

### Safe and quiet
- Data is exchanged invisibly with your guild and with friends on your own friend list – nothing else.
- Nothing is ever posted to chat on its own. Fixed texts only, no free text. Entries are only accepted from the player they're about; stock targets only from officers.
- Lean sync: on login only a short checksum is sent; full data only when something changed. Old data is cleaned up automatically.

### Getting started
1. Log in once on every character.
2. Open each profession window once (and the guild bank, if you can).
3. Ask your guild to install Friendsmith – everything syncs automatically.
4. Type `/fsmith` or click the minimap button. Settings are behind the *Settings* button.

Broker launcher (Titan Panel, ChocolateBar …), minimap button and addon compartment included.
Languages: English and German (follows your game client). Feedback and ideas are very welcome!

## Deutsch

**Wer kann mir etwas Besseres craften – und wem kann ich helfen?**

Friendsmith bringt deine Gilde rund ums Craften zusammen. Es weiß, was alle tragen, welche Rezepte alle haben und was in der Gildenbank liegt – und zeigt, wo ihr euch gegenseitig helfen könnt. Gemacht für alle, die nicht gern fragen: Friendsmith fragt für sie.

Gemacht für WoW Forever und Craftlocked-Gilden, funktioniert aber in jeder Gilde. Freunde außerhalb der Gilde können mitmachen.

- **Upgrades:** wer dir etwas Besseres craften kann – und wem du helfen kannst. Bewertung nach Spec (inkl. Zufallswerten), Leiste am Berufsfenster.
- **Mats & Gildenbank:** zu jedem Upgrade die nötigen Mats mit „du / Gildenbank / fehlt“. Mats-Gesuche, die die ganze Gilde sieht.
- **Gildenlager:** Offiziere legen Mindestmengen fest; alle sehen, was fehlt und wer es herstellen kann.
- **Berufshilfe:** wer welchen Beruf levelt und welche Mats für den nächsten Schritt fehlen – mit OneForAll inklusive Leveling-Route. Dazu: wer das beste Buff-Essen kocht.
- **Stille Wünsche:** Haken setzen – die Gilde sieht von selbst, womit sie dir helfen kann. Helfer bekommen nach dem Login kurze Hinweise.
- **Helfer-Profil & Willkommen:** zeig, wobei du gerne hilfst; Neue in der Gilde werden erkannt und begrüßt.
- **Gilden-Feed & Danke:** Berufsschritte, Level-Meilensteine, erfüllte Gesuche und Dankeschöns – Danke sagen mit einem Klick, auch direkt nach dem Handeln.
- **Sicher & leise:** nichts wird von selbst in den Chat geschrieben, nur feste Texte, Daten nur von Gilde und eigener Freundesliste.

Loslegen: jeden Charakter einmal einloggen, jedes Berufsfenster einmal öffnen, Gilde installieren lassen, `/fsmith`.
